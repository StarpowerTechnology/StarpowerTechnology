WVY Custom Models

Custom models on proven open architectures — Qwen and GLM — tuned for what we care about: multi-agent collaboration, expert reasoning, and efficient information use. The point is not only more parameters. The point is better data, better training signals, and more reasoning per parameter.

● ACTIVE 4B – 100B PARAMS

Start low, punch up

We're a new company and we train like one: 4B–100B parameters, starting at the bottom. Every run teaches us something we carry up the scale. The goal is not the biggest model. The goal is the most useful model per parameter: cleaner datasets, less noise, better reasoning traces, multi-perspective interaction from wvy.world, and expert thinking patterns from the Savvy dataset work.

Training direction

WVY training is aimed at models that can use information to solve problems. Instead of rewarding only immediate answer prediction, we care about reading the available context, organizing it, checking assumptions, and producing a grounded result. That is why the small-model path matters: with a cleaner dataset and sharper training loop, a smaller model can become more reliable for the specific reasoning work we care about.
